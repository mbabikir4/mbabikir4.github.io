Privacy policy

Hermes is a personal assistant used by its owner only. With the owner's consent it reads the owner's own Google Calendar and Google Drive data to answer the owner's questions and prepare the owner's daily brief. Data is processed on the owner's own computer, is not shared with anyone, is not sold, and is not used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page, after which no data is retained. Contact: m2babiker@gmail.com.
